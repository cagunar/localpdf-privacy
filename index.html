LocalPDF — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 June 2026

In short: LocalPDF works entirely on your device. It does not collect, transmit, or share your files or personal data. There are no ads and no tracking.

1. Who we are

LocalPDF (“the app”, “we”, “our”) is a private, offline PDF reader and editor for Android. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information. If you have questions, contact us at the email address listed at the end of this page.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal data. The app does not have an account system, does not require sign-in, and does not send your documents or usage information anywhere.

  • We do not collect your name, email, contacts, or location.
  • We do not collect analytics, usage statistics, or crash data.
  • We do not upload, copy, or transmit your PDF files or their contents.
  • We do not use advertising or third-party tracking SDKs.

3. How your data stays on your device

Everything the app produces stays in local storage on your device unless you explicitly choose to share or export it:

  • Your PDFs remain wherever you keep them. The app reads the files you open and writes new files only to locations you select.
  • Annotations, highlights, notes, signatures, favorites, tags, and reading progress are stored only in the app’s private storage on your device.
  • Backups you create are written only to the file location you choose.

4. Permissions the app uses

  • Camera — used only when you choose “Scan to PDF.” Captured images are written directly into a PDF on your device and are never sent anywhere. The camera is not used at any other time.
  • Google Play billing (com.android.vending.BILLING) — used only if you choose to buy the optional one-time Premium upgrade. This permission communicates with the Google Play app already on your device to process the purchase. It does not grant the app general internet access, and no files or personal data are sent during a purchase.

The app does not request the internet permission for any general networking.

5. Purchases

LocalPDF offers an optional one-time “Premium” purchase that unlocks additional editing tools. Purchases are processed entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details. Google’s handling of your purchase is governed by the Google Privacy Policy. Promotional unlock codes, where offered, are validated on your device and require no account.

6. Sharing and printing

Sharing or printing a document happens only when you initiate it, and is handled by Android’s own system dialog. You choose the destination app or printer. LocalPDF does not control or receive a copy of what you share.

7. Children’s privacy

LocalPDF does not collect personal data from anyone, including children. The app is suitable for general audiences and contains no ads or data collection.

8. Data security

Because your data never leaves your device, it is protected by your device’s own security (screen lock, encryption, and OS protections). The app also offers an optional biometric/PIN app lock for an additional layer of protection.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
